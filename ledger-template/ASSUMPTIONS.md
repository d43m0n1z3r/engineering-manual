# Assumptions and Open Questions

| ID | Statement or question | Impact | Owner | Validation plan | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

ID format: `ASM-###` for assumptions, `OQ-###` for open questions.

Status values: OPEN, CONFIRMED, REFUTED, EXPIRED, SUPERSEDED.

Rules:

- An assumption affecting security, data handling, cost, or scope requires human
  confirmation before dependent work proceeds.
- Confirmed assumptions become decisions or requirements; refuted assumptions
  are closed with the impact note.
- Do not invent values; offer labelled options for the human to choose.
