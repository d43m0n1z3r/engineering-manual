## Source task and objective

**Threat modeling and abuse-case analysis.**

## Execution instructions

Procedure: (1) Define scope and assets. (2) Build DFDs with **trust boundaries**. (3) Enumerate entry points and the attack surface. (4) Apply STRIDE per element (and LINDDUN for privacy). (5) Add abuse cases from Phase 01 and business-logic abuse (race conditions, workflow skipping, quota abuse, and price tampering). (6) Rate threats (DREAD or CVSS-like, or risk matrix). (7) Assign mitigations to controls and tests. (8) Record residual risk and acceptance. (9) Map to MITRE ATT&CK/CAPEC where useful.

## Expected outputs

Threat Model (`THR-###`), Attack Surface Inventory, Residual Risk Register

## Validation

All trust-boundary crossings have threats considered. Each High/Critical threat has a mitigation with a verification test ID.
