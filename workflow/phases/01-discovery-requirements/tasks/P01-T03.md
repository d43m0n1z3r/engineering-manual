## Source task and objective

**Elicit business, functional, and non-functional requirements.**

## Execution instructions

Derive business requirements (outcomes), then functional requirements ("The system SHALL…" with unique IDs). Derive NFRs by quality attribute: performance (p95 latency, throughput), availability (SLO, RTO, RPO), scalability (users, data growth), security, privacy, accessibility, compliance, observability, maintainability, compatibility, and cost. Every NFR MUST be quantified (for example "p95 < 300 ms at 500 rps"). If the stakeholders cannot quantify it, record an assumption and propose a default.
