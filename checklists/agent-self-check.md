## 19.10 Agent self-check (before claiming completion)

1. Did I verify the preconditions with evidence?
2. Did I do exactly what the task said, and did I record any deviation?
3. Did I run the validation myself, and is the output real (not remembered or inferred)?
4. Is each claim backed by an EVD ID?
5. Did I state what was *not* verified?
6. Did I update the docs, the RTM, and the state?
7. Did I check for secrets and the sensitive data in my outputs?
8. Did I stay within my permission tier and the scope?
9. Is the handoff reproducible by someone with no context?
10. Have I asked for an independent review rather than self-approving?
11. When Graphify is configured, did I query the knowledge graph before the change and refresh it (`--update`, or the configured watch/hook) after it, alongside the ledger update?
